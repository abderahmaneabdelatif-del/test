Frequently Asked Questions

Is this detectable by Warden (anti-cheat)? ▼

Max Combat Assistant does not read or write game memory. We use a pixel-based engine and external input, designed to keep the footprint small.

Can I use my license on a different PC? ▼

No—keys are HWID-bound. For a new machine or major hardware change, open a ticket in Discord for a manual reset.

How does crypto payment delivery work? ▼

We use NOWPayments. After network confirmation (often 1–3 minutes), the server issues your key—check email and the License Portal.

What is the difference between Monthly, 3-Month, and Per-Spec? ▼

Monthly ($30/mo): All 39 specs unlocked for one month. 3-Month ($75): Same access but for 3 months at a discounted rate of $25/mo. Per Spec ($15/spec/mo): Choose individual specs and pay only for those you play.

Do you offer refunds? ▼

Due to the nature of digital license delivery, refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis. Contact support on Discord within 24 hours of purchase.
